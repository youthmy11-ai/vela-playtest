# Vela Mini Games — Android playtest

Three short games by Lucci Digital Studio: **Rhythm Ring**, **Bloom Grid** and **Star Drift**.

The Android closed-test release is active. Public self-service invitations are being prepared and checked; joining is not open on this page yet. This page will contain the actual verified Google Play steps when invitations are ready.

## Meet the games

- **Rhythm Ring:** tap when the moving spark reaches the glowing arc.
- **Bloom Grid:** place flowers and connect four or more matching flowers.
- **Star Drift:** drag your glider, collect golden stars and avoid red sparks.

The volunteer test is free and supports Android 8.0 or later. Gameplay works offline after installation. No separate game account or subscription is required. The current build uses demo ads where optional privacy choices permit advertising.

## Taking part

Once invitations open, use the Google account on your Android phone and follow the Google Play instructions on the playtest page. Please remain opted in for at least 14 consecutive days and try all three games over several sessions.

Honest gameplay feedback is welcome. No purchase, public rating or review is requested, and no testing swap is promised.

## Reporting a problem

Private Google Play testing feedback is preferred. A public issue can also describe the game, device model, Android version, steps, expected result and actual result. Keep emails, account information and other personal details out of public issues or screenshots.
