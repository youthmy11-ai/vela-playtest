BEFORE YOU PLAY

A few useful details.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Gameplay works offline after installation. You’ll need an internet connection to join the test, install updates, and send Google Play feedback.

What is included in the test?

Rhythm Ring, Bloom Grid and Star Drift in one Android app. High scores are saved on your device. Sound is optional.

Does this build have ads?

The current test build uses demo ads where optional privacy choices permit advertising. This is an unpaid volunteer playtest; there is no subscription.

How can I report a problem?

Use private testing feedback in Google Play. Include the game name, your device model, Android version, and the steps that caused the problem. Please keep your Google email and other personal details out of public comments.
